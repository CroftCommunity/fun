Gallery of Worlds

The icons and splashes already are an identity — dark, luminous, one saturated world per game. So the chrome stops competing: it goes near-black and near-silent, and borrows each game's own accent as you move. The art is the design; the chrome is the gallery lighting. The home page opens on what you were doing, because a returning player is the common case.
